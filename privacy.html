Privacy Policy

Farsi Daily · Effective July 27, 2026

Privacy in plain language

Farsi Daily does not require an account. It does not contain advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics. Learning progress is stored on the device where the app is used.

Information stored on your device

The app stores saved words, review history, lesson completion, streak information, script-practice results, and settings. This information is used only to provide the learning and review features.

Information we collect

Farsi Daily does not operate a user database and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, payment information, advertising identifiers, or lesson history.

Pronunciation audio

Curriculum words and sentences use audio files bundled with Farsi Daily. For an uncommon phrase that does not have a bundled recording, the app may use a genuine Persian voice provided by the device. Farsi Daily does not send pronunciation text to a third-party text-to-speech service.

Deleting your data

You can erase all saved words, lesson progress, script progress, review history, and settings from the Settings screen by choosing Reset all learning progress.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because it does not provide accounts, social features, advertising, or analytics.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest published version.

Support

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Farsi Daily support page.
